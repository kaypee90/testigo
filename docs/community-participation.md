# Proposed community participation

**Draft for discussion, not completed co-design.** No organization has been contacted about
this proposal or agreed to participate. No interviews, workshops or deployments are claimed.
The starter taxonomy and software are revisable hypotheses (G6, FR-INT-2, FR-PAT-5).

## Resumen en español

Proponemos decidir con participantes y una posible organización colaboradora si la herramienta
es útil, qué información debería recoger y qué no debe publicarse. Participar sería voluntario,
con compensación y apoyos de accesibilidad acordados antes de empezar. Se usarían escenarios
ficticios; nadie tendría que revelar una experiencia personal. Decidir no desplegar la
herramienta o no publicar datos también sería un resultado válido.

## First-stage proposal

1. Map existing reporting, advocacy and case-management practices with local organizations.
   Ask what works already and whether software would address a useful gap.
2. Agree a small, compensated participant group with varied access needs and perspectives.
   Participants choose whether to bring an advocate. Offer accessible, low-bandwidth and
   non-code ways to respond, and a way to decline without explaining why.
3. Test fictional scenarios, consent language and taxonomy labels. Record suggestions and
   disagreement without recording personal harm narratives. Obtain separate permission for
   any session notes or quotations; compensation must not depend on agreeing with the proposal.
4. Review the prototype together and decide what to keep, change or stop. Publish only a
   participant-approved, non-identifying summary of decisions and limitations.

Recruitment partners, session count, payment rates, languages and accessibility support are
**VALIDATE** items, not agreed arrangements. See Q16–Q18 in `open-questions.md`.

## Proposed decision rights, to agree before a pilot

| Decision | Proposed authority |
|---|---|
| Whether to use the tool and which needs it serves | Participants and the deploying organization jointly; maintainers provide feasibility evidence |
| Optional fields, taxonomy and consent wording | Participant review and explicit agreement by the deploying organization |
| A person's participation and optional data uses | That person; participation can be declined or withdrawn under an agreed process |
| Pilot readiness and pause/stop decisions | Joint review of security, legal, accessibility and operating evidence; unresolved serious concerns pause the pilot |
| Publication | Separate reporter consent, participant-defined disclosure rules and reviewer approval; any missing condition prevents release |
| Software changes | Maintainers remain responsible for implementation and explain rejected proposals; technical authority does not substitute for participant consent |

Before any real data, specify withdrawal/deletion procedures and their limits, who handles
disagreements and complaints, and an independent escalation route. Publication notices must
explain licensing, downstream reuse and why already downloaded public copies may not be recoverable.
If a safe dataset cannot be produced, share synthetic examples and methodology instead.

## Evidence and sustainability

Agree success measures with participants: comprehension of the notice, ability to decline
optional fields, usefulness of analyst suggestions, time required and maintenance burden.
Record adverse feedback and unresolved disagreements, not just favorable comments.
Start hosting, ownership, maintenance funding and handover planning in the first stage.
The approved draft budget reserves $3,000 for participation/accessibility; obtain actual
costs before committing to a session plan.
