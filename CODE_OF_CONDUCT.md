# Code of conduct

Testigo exists to help people who have been harmed by technology be heard. We expect the same care
from everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews, chats and events.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in their second
  language. Spanish, Portuguese and English are all welcome.
- Assume good faith, disagree about ideas rather than people, and accept that maintainers make the
  final call on scope.
- Keep in mind who this project is for. Never share, ask for or speculate about real reports,
  real victims or real people's personal data. Examples and test data are invented.
- Credit other people's work.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language, including about gender,
  sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, Indigenous identity, migration status, religion, age
  or appearance.
- Publishing someone's private information (doxxing), or pressuring anyone to reveal it.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers privately, through the same private channel as security
reports ([`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break this code, in proportion to
what happened. Maintainers who break it are held to the same standard.

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public when someone represents the project.
